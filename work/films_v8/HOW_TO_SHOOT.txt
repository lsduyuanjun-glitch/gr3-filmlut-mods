Films v8 = v7 + per-film warm-colour correction (CCA1 post-gamma 3x3). Everything else identical to v7
(film curves/matrices, RAW NR off up to ISO 8000). Standard and all other modes are unchanged.

Flash work/films_v8/fwdc239b.bin (SHA-256 150fe109...44e7727). This replaces the red/blue-swap probe.

Chart test (~10 min, same setup as photo-ma5):
1. Monitor: show hue_chart_3840x2160.png (or your resolution) full screen. Night Shift / True Tone / HDR / eye saver off.
   Let it sit ~1 min. Room dim, no light on the screen.
2. Camera on a tripod or books, straight at the screen, all 4 white corner squares in frame.
3. M mode, ISO 200, f/5.6, ~1/30 (white squares bright, not blinking), WB Daylight, DR / shadow correction OFF,
   clarity/shading 0, RAW+JPG, focus slightly off, 2 s self-timer.
4. Without moving anything, one shot each: Standard, Vivid (Ektar), Retro (Gold), HDR Tone (Pro 400H),
   Cross Process (Portra), Standard again.
5. Put the 6 RAW+JPG pairs in ~/dev/gr3-firmware/photo-v8/.
Plus, if you like, a few real photos with skin / orange / autumn colours in the film modes.
