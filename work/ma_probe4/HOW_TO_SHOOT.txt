MultiAxial probe round 4 - how to shoot (temporary firmware, flash films v2 back afterwards)

1. Monitor: show hue_chart_<your monitor resolution>.png full screen (Samsung G81SF: 3840x2160), no other windows,
   mouse cursor moved off the chart. Night Shift / True Tone off, HDR off, Eye Saver / dynamic brightness /
   black equalizer off. Any normal picture mode is fine (accuracy doesn't matter, stability does).
   Let the chart sit on screen ~1 min before the first shot.
2. Room: dim, no lamp or window light falling on the screen (pitch dark not needed, just constant).
3. Camera on a tripod or a stack of books, straight at the screen, chart filling most of the frame (all 4 white corner squares visible).
4. Settings: M mode, ISO 200, f/5.6 (or so), shutter so the white squares are bright but not blinking (try 1/30). WB Daylight.
   DR / highlight correction OFF, shadow correction OFF, clarity/shading 0. RAW+JPG. Focus slightly OFF (avoids moire). 2s self timer.
5. Without moving the camera or changing exposure, take one shot in each Image Control, quickly:
   Standard, Vivid, Positive Film, Negative Film, Retro, HDR Tone, Cross Processing, Standard again (skip Bleach Bypass)  (8 shots, all 8 needed this time)
   (the second Standard shows whether the screen dimmed during the series)
6. Put the 8 RAW+JPG pairs in ~/dev/gr3-firmware/photo-ma4/
7. Expected: some modes may look darker, tinted or colour-shifted, others identical to Standard. That is the test working.
   If a mode gives an error, a black frame or a frozen camera, note which one, power-cycle, and continue.
8. Before shooting, check the update really applied: switch to Vivid and back to Standard in live view.
   This build sets untested hardware control bits, so a mode may also show garbled colours, a frozen
   live view or an error. If that happens: switch back to Standard (or pull the battery), note the mode, continue.
